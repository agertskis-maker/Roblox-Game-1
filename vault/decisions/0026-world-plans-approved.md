Adam approved both plans with the answers he wrote in section 1 of the brief. They are copied into each plan's "Approval requested" section, with the date. Short form:

## Lead's choices (all reversible)
1. **Summit has no P1/P2/P3 tags.** Its inventory table has no priority column; its 8.4 lists build phases P0 to P7. Reading used: everything in Adam's approved answers is required and built in phase order P1 to P6 (P7 is verification); the plan's "Optional" lines are the conditional P3 tier, built only inside the ceilings. Dusty keeps the inventory tags P1, P2 and P3 as written; its 8.4 phases give the order.
2. **Summit visible-triangle ceiling.** The plan sets none ("log the baseline first"). Used: measure the baseline, then test against 60,000 visible triangles, the figure the Downtown plan proposed and the Dusty plan cites; unique triangles stay at or below the B2 kit's 56,751 as the plan says. Labelled as the lead's number in every table.
3. **New kit folder.** The new World meshes go to `Car-Hop-Art\downtown-kit-worlds-v2\` (mesh JSON for the EditableMesh preview, OBJ for the upload). The old `downtown-kit-worlds\` stays untouched so the "before" views stay reproducible and the HUD playground backdrop does not change.
4. **Memory floor.** The brief says to pause heavy work under 4 GB free; Adam told the Speedway session on 2026-10-28 to keep going and just report it. This task follows his later words: report dips, do not stop, and do not run Blender next to Studio when memory is very low.
5. **Folders changed.** `src/lookdev/Downtown/**`, `assets/downtown/kit/**`, `playground/downtown.project.json` only if needed, `vault/**`. `AGENTS.md` does not list `src/lookdev` or `assets/downtown` in its lane table; the plans and the City README call them Lane 3 look-dev, and neither is in Brian R's or Brian L's paths.

## Build results and later choices (lead, 2026-10-29; all reversible)
6. **Dusty light time: ClockTime 15.5.** Tests at 14.2, 15.5 and 16.5: road:verge 2.14:1 at 14.2 and 2.56:1 at 16.5 (the plan target is 1.8:1); 15.5 is a compromise, not separately measured. The sky stays the engine default (no uploaded skybox, no Sky object); Atmosphere and the colour grade could not shift it without washing the frame grey.
7. **Summit road retune (approved test, S12).** The Round 2 slate (90,98,114) measured 2.67 to 2.95 to 1 against the snow in Studio stills; `asphalt`, `asphalt_gutter`, `asphalt_patch`, `asphalt_side` in the Summit column are now (72,80,98), (60,68,84), (68,76,92), (66,74,90): 4.3 to 4.5 to 1. The warm sun tint was not applied.
8. **Dusty animated parts held at 30.** Counting every part moved in a 0.1 second tick plus tumbleweeds and a running dust devil, Dusty reached 41 with the wheel (11 parts) and the pennant strings (16) both on. The wheel now runs within 240 studs and the strings within 190 (the plan allows 600 and 350 at most); they are about 500 studs apart, so the maximum is 30.
9. **Gorge ground.** Roblox clamps a part to 2,048 studs a side; the gorge's four replacement slabs are built from parts of at most 2,048 (16 parts and the floor). The gorge is the approved 124 studs wide (half-width 62) and 416 long under the bridge: a pit the bridge spans, not a canyon cutting the whole landscape.
10. **Audio in videos.** Studio Edit mode plays no sound, and Play cannot hold the EditableMesh previews. Each World video is therefore the Edit-mode run with the World's music loop (the same `Music.luau` module) recorded in a Play session laid under it, labelled as such. The loop has no sync event. Both mixes measure about -34 LUFS integrated, true peak -20 and -21 dBTP (`Measure-Loudness.ps1`, EBU R128 K-weighted).
11. **Skipped (P3 and proposals).** Dusty: lookout shelf across the gorge, drive-through arch (proposal), scenery vehicles (Brian L's later item). Summit: ski lift Option M (proposal), the slow observatory dome turn (optional).
12. **Not tested.** Studio Play and the phone and tablet frames (Play cannot hold the previews), the two-lap wrap at 6,400 studs, the desaturated hazard test, Play ceilings and draw calls. Nothing was uploaded.
13. **DEVIATION from Adam's S8 answer (landmark spacing).** In the Summit Lodge district (681 studs) the order observatory (s2571), snowmen row (s2641), lodge (s3009) is built, but the two minors are 70 studs apart (approved 300 to 440; plan: minors at least 250 from a major, which holds). A 260-stud gap placed the observatory outside the district and broke the order. The planner's `landmark-order` check tests order only. Options for Adam: accept 70, lengthen the district, or turn the snowmen row into a prop. Not silently accepted.
14. **Summit snow berms** (`berms` chain rule) now have 90 to 130 stud gaps: with proxy traffic and the chase car the Summit run reached 484 live parts against about 440; now 441. Visible triangles peak 45,067.
15. **Dusty bridge under-frames** hang `underDrop = 11` (was 8.5): their top struts sat at deck level and poked through the road as bars across the lanes.
16. **Known limit:** the gorge ground ring (`GORGE_REACH = 2112`) assumes a gorge that is roughly axis-aligned with the ground tiles (true on the straight Dusty route); a rotated gorge would need a wider ring. Planner checks are narrower than the plan in places (hazard-clear covers mass, plant and edge pieces within 120 studs; no 10-stud boulder offset check); the gorge check is now a real test.
