# 0035: Launch Pads become Sky Launch Platforms; five bolts

Date: 2026-11-02. Owner: Adam (Lane 3).

**Decision.** Every Launch Pad is one round platform floating 170 studs over a cheap diorama of its World, with three skins on one measured layout. A run has 5 bolts, 1 per hop.

**Why these choices.**
- 170 studs, not higher: at 300 the Atmosphere hid the World and the platform seemed to float in fog.
- The World below uses the group-owned kits, so nothing new needs uploading.
- Stand-in circles replace the kiosk prompts, so a phone player just walks in. Each opens once per visit to the circle.
- START hands the camera to the gantry for the countdown. Reduce Motion keeps the view and shows the same lights.
- The pad leaves the world on green, so a run can never see it.
- The Cars turntable shows the line-up, one car every 5 seconds, so the kiosk does not look the same as the others. The small roof car and the Trails car still show your pick.
- The tutorial run has 8 bolts, so a new player can still reach a first claim. This is client preview logic, like the rest of `RunStamina`.
- The tutorial's title stays "Go to Downtown" in Downtown and reads "To the ramp!" in any other pad.

**Limits.** The Downtown platform is about 30 parts over the Round 3 limit. No phone measurement exists. Ambient sounds were picked from the Creator Store by title and have not been listened to.
