# 0038: The Starter Pack includes a Rare car (Brawler)

Date: 2026-11-03. Owner: Adam (repository owner).
Status: decided by Adam. A propose-first item (paid items, `AGENTS.md`): Claude set out the benefit, cost and risk below in chat, and Adam answered "Yes, add the car".

## Decision

The Starter Pack (PREVIEW 79 Robux) contains Brawler, a Rare car, plus $5,000 Cash, Rookie Flame paint and Starter Stripe decal. One per player.

Adam's words (2026-11-03): "instead of changing the art add a car that looks cool from the rare rarity for players to start out w", then "Yes, add the car".

## Why

Two fresh Shop reviewers said the Starter Pack art (a flame sports car) makes a child think a car is included. Adam chose to make the art true by including a car, instead of drawing new art.

## Which car

Brawler: a teal muscle car, Rare, from Downtown (the first World), so a new player can race it straight away. Bolt, the other Downtown Rare, was not chosen, because Brian L's roster flags "Bolt" as an unchanged real car model name, which a paid offer should not feature.

## What it changes

- Brian L's rule "cars and editions are never sold for Robux" gains one exception: this one car, inside this one pack. Editions are still never sold, and no other car is sold.
- The answers for Brian L (`vault/design/specs/economy-v1-answers-from-adam.md`) say so. The grant (Brawler into `Cars`, Normal, 0 copies, once per player) is his server's, inside `ProcessReceipt`, like the rest of the pack.

## Costs and risks (as told to Adam before he decided)

- **Pay-to-start-faster:** a Rare car earns $250 a lap against $10 for the free Common Loaner. A buyer starts ahead.
- **Less to find:** Brawler no longer has to be found in a run by a buyer. It still counts toward the Downtown 9 and the World unlock.
- **Needs Brian L to agree,** because it changes his economy rule.
- **The VIP and Expanded Paddock art still show cars** that those passes do not include. That still needs new art or a label.

## Where it shows

Shop, Featured: the Starter Pack's "Includes" panel shows the real Brawler model with a RARE chip as its first tile. Nothing can be bought: a price press still says Coming soon.

## Revisit if

Brian L does not agree. Or playtests show buyers racing far ahead of free players. Or a Roblox policy check says otherwise.
