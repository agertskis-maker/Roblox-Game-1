## Lane 2 Luck Station in the Car Hop Beta - Brian L, with Claude Code

Built on Brian L's Luck Station preview (`feature/lane2-economy-preview`, commit `49c13a2`) and run tweaks (`3342788`), on top of `integration/beta-4`, the code the live Car Hop Beta runs. Branch `feature/lane2-luck-in-beta`, not merged.

- In the beta now: a Luck Station stand on the Downtown Launch Pad (hold E to buy the next level with the beta's Cash), 10 PREVIEW levels (x1 to x2.25, $5K to $80B, `src/shared/Catalog/LuckTable.luau`), a beta-only `LuckLevel` save field (1 to 10, repaired on load), and a per-player sign with level, luck and next price. No new remote: the ProximityPrompt fires on the server, which prices and checks the buy.
- Each run's traffic is built with that luck on the server (`BetaRun.start`) and the StartRun reply carries it to the client. Also from the run tweaks: every traffic car Normal size, Rare/Epic/Legendary weights x2, Epic and Legendary sparkles, beacon and ring.
- Tested in a downloaded copy of the live Car Hop Beta (`CarHop-Real-Luck-Test.rbxl`, place ID 0, Mock saves): every existing beta self-test passes, the new `luck` self-test passes 14/14 (buy, refusal with the missing Cash, cooldown, top level, runs at level 7 and 1). Over 100 seeded 2 km runs: level 1 Legendary 1.7%, Epic 8.1%, Rare 31.8%; level 10 Legendary 3.3%, Epic 14.8%, Rare 61.8% (Commons floor at 20%); all 5,000+ cars Normal size. Screenshots: the stand and sign on the Downtown pad, an Epic car with sparkles in a run. `check.ps1` exit 0.
- Not tested: pressing E by hand (the buy was tested through the self-test, not a real key press), a Legendary on screen, effects in motion (stills only, no video), Dusty Highway and Summit Pass pads (the stand is on Downtown only), phones.
- Touches files outside Lane 2's folders, for review: Brian R's `src/client/Run/RunController.luau` and `src/shared/Run` (luck option, run tweaks), Adam's `src/server/Beta`, `src/client/Beta/BetaRun.client.luau` and `src/shared/Beta/BetaConfig.luau`. Adam's Round 7 (`integration/beta-5`) has its own Lucky Clover; one of the two luck systems has to be chosen before both reach the beta.
- `LuckLevel` and the prices are a preview: the real save field and Catalog need "Agreed" from all three lanes.
