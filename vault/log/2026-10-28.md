# 2026-10-28

## Lane 3 Dusty Highway and Summit Pass build (Adam, with Claude Code; a Sonnet lead with two Sonnet kit workers)
Brief [[worlds-dusty-summit-build]], ledger [[worlds-dusty-summit-ledger]], decision [[0026-world-plans-approved]], message for Brian R [[worlds-dusty-summit-for-brian-r]]. Branch `feature/lane3-worlds-dusty-summit`, started from `feature/lane3-hud-downtown-lookdev` (its leftover Session 1 work was pushed first as three commits, at Adam's "push its work then start"). Nothing was merged, published or uploaded. Review page: `Car-Hop-Art\review\worlds-dusty-summit\index.html`.

**What was built (look-dev only, Studio Edit previews).** WorldPlan V2 (a per-World layer in `src/lookdev/Downtown/City`: `WorldPlan.luau`, `WorldFeatures.luau`, changes in `Assembler`, `Sequencer`, `Catalog`, `WorldLife`, `Proxies`, and the two World data files). Downtown is untouched. Dusty Highway: five districts with the clay verge, sandstone masses, plant families, the thick arch plus the little arch, the true gorge under the Canyon Bridge, and the approved motion (windmill wheel, two pennant strings, shimmer strip). Summit Pass: backdrop first (far massifs, ridge curtains, one horn), the frozen lake, the glacier tunnel gallery, the ski lift with a static crossing, the stepped lodge with the festival stage, static garlands, skiers and skaters. Two Sonnet workers (each reported claude-sonnet-5-5) wrote the Blender recipes `kit_dusty2.py` and `kit_summit2.py`; the lead reviewed their gallery sheets, wired the data and ran everything in Studio.

**Measured** (Studio Edit preview, desktop, laptop on AC; mean frame time 16.7 ms in both Worlds): Dusty peaks at 24,080 visible triangles, 91 unique meshes, 236 live parts, 30 animated parts (ceilings 35,000, 110, 400, 30). Summit peaks at 48,089 visible triangles, 124 unique meshes (36,779 unique triangles), 402 live parts, 55 animated parts, 0 visible transparent surfaces, five smoke emitters (ceilings 60,000, about 130, about 440, 100, 5). Play ceilings and draw calls were not measured.

**Found by running and fixed:** Roblox clamps a part to 2,048 studs a side, so the 4,096-stud gorge slabs left bare ground and a "sea" of sky beside the Canyon Bridge (now cut into parts); the animated parts of Dusty reached 41 against a ceiling of 30 (the wheel and pennant ranges were narrowed to 240 and 190 studs so they are never on together); the Summit road measured 2.67 to 2.95 to 1 against the snow (plan: 3 to 1), so the road swatches were darkened about 20 percent (now 4.3 to 4.5 to 1); the harness' own "Studio hover outline" showed in the first videos until the mouse was parked over empty sky.

**Choices made by the lead (all reversible, in [[0026-world-plans-approved]]):** Dusty ClockTime 15.5; the Summit sun tint was not applied; the new kit folder `downtown-kit-worlds-v2`; the 60,000-triangle Summit figure; the clock tower label wording.

**Not done, and why.** Uploads: the Car Hop group does not exist yet (Adam, 2026-10-28), so no mesh was uploaded; used-only OBJ packages (94 and 125 meshes) are ready in `Car-Hop-Art\downtown-kit-worlds-v2-upload`. Studio Play and the phone and tablet frames: Play cannot hold the EditableMesh previews, so nothing was tested in Play. Sound: Studio Edit mode plays no sound (a plain `Sound` stayed silent in a loopback recording), so each video is the Edit run with the World's music loop recorded in a Play session laid under it; the loop has no event to sync, and the mix measures about -34 LUFS (the game's own quiet music level). Skipped P3 and proposal items: Dusty lookout shelf, drive-through arch, scenery vehicles; Summit ski lift Option M, the slow observatory dome turn. Sky: the engine default sky is saturated cyan and could not be tuned without an uploaded skybox.
