# Car rebuild: separate body, glass, lights and a detailed back

Brian L (car looks owner, decision 0015) asked on 2026-11-08 for every car to have black windows that stay black under paint, paint that changes only the body, and detailed backs (taillights that read, exhausts, no flat or Play-Doh rear), uploads under his account as before.

Why a rebuild: the 27 showroom cars were made with Studio's AI mesh tool as one mesh each, with glass, lights and trim painted into one texture. No Blender source exists (searched Brian L's Google Drive, the mounted G: drive, Documents, Downloads, Desktop and D:). Studio's AI segment tool could not separate the glass (it is texture, not shape). The colour-role rule in [[rarity-style]] ("Recolouring changes role colours only") had never been met.

How: each car was generated again with Studio's AI mesh tool, with explicit parts, about 10,000 triangles at most, in the old car's size and look (from [[showroom-roster]]): body, windows (Cruiser and Beach Bug: windscreen and seats), headlights, taillights, exhaust or rear bumper, front wheels, rear wheels, plus one extra where the car has one (spoiler, roof rack, pizza sign, surfboard, roll bar, pop-up roof). Each was then tidied in Brian L's private copy: clean part names, the body alone in `body/body_geom`, the pivot at the bottom centre facing -Z (aimed by the wheel pairs; Street Kid turned by hand; light parts renamed by where they really sit on Apex, Zip, Thunder, Trailblazer, Minnow and Family Hauler), and a check for stray full-size shells (the trial Apex's trim shell was removed; Brawler and Thunder were generated again).

Editions: Gold, Diamond and Rainbow are the new Normal car with the old edition's body finish (Gold metal, Diamond glass) and its effects (sparkles, glow, the Rainbow shimmer and script), all on the body part only; the Rainbow highlight adorns the body only. Attributes `CarName`, `Variant`, `Rarity` are kept; `RebuiltOn = 2026-11-08` is added.

Paint: `RealCar` paints a rebuilt car (it has a `windows` or `windscreen` part) by replacing the body part's colour; one-piece cars keep the see-through wash.

Not checked: the new meshes' asset privacy and Open Use (generated in Brian L's Studio session; whether a group-owned published game can load them is not verified), phone cost with many cars on screen, every car's back by eye (all were checked by numbers; Legendaries, Epics, Rally Dog, Trailblazer, Beach Bug, Bolt, Monarch and Pizza Runner by eye), the run traffic (it uses stand-in cars, not these models).
