# Vehicle roster: 24 launch vehicles (proposed, pending Adam's review)

> [!warning] Proposal
> Revised 2026-10-20 from the Stop 1 roster with Adam's corrections: the Legendaries now outshine the Epics; most cars, and nearly all Rare and up, are 1990s to today; every Rare looks sportier than every Uncommon in its World; vans, buses and big rigs stay Common or Uncommon. Families and quirks are unchanged (outline v2.2 section 07). Per World: 2 Common, 2 Uncommon, 2 Rare, 1 Epic, 1 Legendary; each Legendary stays in the Special/Magnet slot (Adam). Style rules: [[rarity-style]]. Names follow `AGENTS.md` "Vehicles"; risk notes are judgement, not legal advice, and no clearance is established. Nothing here is in the Catalog.

Quirks: Compact Nimble, Sedan Steady, Pickup/4x4 Rugged, Van/RV Cool Runner, Motorcycle Slipstream, Truck/Bus/Plow Plow, Sports Long Jump, Special Magnet.

## Electric extras (proposed 2026-10-24, Brian L)

That breaks the "8 per World, one per family" shape and needs Adam and Brian R to agree. Moving them to another World or swapping out an existing car would keep the shape.
