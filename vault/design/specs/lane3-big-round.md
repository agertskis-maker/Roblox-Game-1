## M1. Flicker and overlap sweep across the whole game

**Adam:** PIT ROW and many other buildings have parts overlapping inside one another that flicker when you walk past. Search the entire game and fix all of it. Also: paths and floors that glitch into each other, corners missing, jagged seams (screenshots 08, 09, 10).

**M1-01 Build a reusable checker** (Lane 3 code, for example `src/island/Hub/Preview/OverlapChecks.luau`, plus a shared helper usable for the Worlds look-dev and the Stunt Circuit). It runs in Studio (Edit or Play) through the Studio MCP and reports:
- **Coplanar overlaps (flicker):** pairs of visible BaseParts (Transparency < 1) whose faces lie in the same plane (normals parallel, plane distance under about 0.02 studs) and whose face rectangles overlap by more than a small area. Handle Part, WedgePart, CornerWedgePart, Cylinder ends and MeshPart bounding faces as approximations. Use the spatial query API with a bounding-box prefilter so it runs in reasonable time on thousands of parts. Report position, the two instance paths, the overlap area and a severity.
- **Near-coplanar fights:** faces within about 0.05 studs that will flicker at distance.
- **Gaps and steps between joined floor pieces:** adjacent floor surfaces with a visible gap or a height step.
- **Floating and tipped props:** decoration whose base is more than about 0.2 studs above or below the ground beneath it, or tilted more than a few degrees when it should be upright (benches, lamps, signs, barrels, towers, poles).
- **Inside checks** used by M2: flowers outside their bed's real shape, beds overlapping paths, benches inside beds, fruit outside its tree canopy, trees intersecting structures.
- Output: a readable report (printed and saved as a text file in the task folder) with counts per category and the top offenders, plus a way to place temporary marker beams at each finding for screenshots.
- Choose thresholds yourself; calibrate them on known-bad spots (the PIT ROW building, screenshots 08 to 10) and known-good spots so the checker finds real problems without drowning in false positives.

**M1-02 Fix at the source.** Every fix goes into the recipe or kit code that generates the geometry (island recipes, Raceway kit, Worlds look-dev, Stunt Circuit), never as hand edits in Studio (a rebuild would wipe them). Typical fixes: merge or remove hidden duplicate parts; offset decorative layers (paint lines, decals, trims, signs) by a small, consistent amount above or in front of their base; build layered floors on defined height layers (for example grass 0, path +0.05, plaza +0.10, paint +0.02 above its surface); make butt joints instead of overlaps for path segments; cut corners cleanly or cap with a rounded end.
**M1-03 Scope:** island (park, plaza, plots at Raceway levels 1 to 12, portals, Garage, Stunt gate, bridges, river edges), Downtown, Dusty Highway, Summit Pass, the Stunt Circuit (M17), and the PIT ROW building. Imported meshes that flicker internally are fixed in Blender (re-export) or replaced.
**M1-04 Done when:** the checker reports zero flicker-severity findings in every scene (or a justified, listed exception), a slow walk-through in Studio Play past PIT ROW, the Garage, the plaza and each plot shows no flicker, and the checker itself is committed with a short how-to-run note in the ledger.

Process bounded batches and report scan duration, candidate count and narrow-phase count. Do not compare every object with every other object.
