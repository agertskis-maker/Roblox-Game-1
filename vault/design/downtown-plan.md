## 3. Segment needs (proposals for Brian R)

Straight, gentle curve (radius about 300 studs), intersection (cross street 30 wide), bridge,
construction (lane closure with barriers and a crane on a lot), underpass and overpass (the
elevated rail in C is a decorative overpass with at least 40 studs clearance). A drawbridge or a
gap would be a gameplay mechanic: a proposal for Brian R only, not part of this plan.

## 6. Traffic budget

The outline gives about 18 visible traffic cars, 20 to 40 parts per car, a 30 fps phone target
with StreamingEnabled. The look-development scenes had no traffic beyond one proxy car (and the
HUD playground's 6 proxies); headroom for 18 cars must be reserved in any scenery budget.
