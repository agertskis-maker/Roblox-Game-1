# Lane 3 delegation (standing rules from Adam)

Set by Adam (Lane 3 owner) on 2026-10-22 in the Task 3 brief. **Every Lane 3 session reads this right after `AGENTS.md`.** Where this note and `AGENTS.md` disagree, `AGENTS.md` wins unless this note is a newer, explicit grant from Adam.

## You may decide on your own
Record each decision in the review page's "Decisions I made for you" table.
- Visual and layout details inside Lane 3 that stay within approved decisions and pass the checks: corridors, reservations, portal sight, falls view, walk routes and the phone frame budget.
- Asset sources:
  - free Creator Store audio from Roblox's licensed partners (for example Pro Sound Effects, APM);
  - your own original meshes and images.
- Uploads under peskaface, logged in the asset manifest, while game ownership is undecided.
- Inserting sounds for testing, as long as you tell Adam to listen to them before they count as final.
- Fixing anything your own checks or the independent reviewer flag, within scope.
- Local saves of the test place (File > Save to File, or Download a Copy, to a new baseline file), never to Roblox.

## Stop and ask Adam before
- Changing another lane's code or a shared contract: movement, data, economy, plot assignment, server size, replicated unlock state.
- Publishing, saving to Roblox, merging into `main` (board-only commits per the branch policy are fine), or touching the shared Team Create place.
- Spending Robux, or uploading under any owner other than peskaface.
- Setting any asset to Open Use, or anything else that cannot be undone.
- Deleting or overwriting a teammate's work.
- Changing an approved layout decision beyond what a prompt authorizes.
- Monetization, saved data, or economy numbers.

## Always
- The Studio lock (`Car-Hop-Art/STUDIO_LOCK.txt`).
- The 4 GB free-memory floor: pause heavy work and wait if it drops; don't push through.
- Honest test reporting, in three groups: tested in Studio, read in code only, and untested.
- One review page at the end.

## Before stopping
Run an independent review with a fresh subagent that has not seen your work:
1. Give it the requirement list and the screenshots and videos.
2. Ask it to find anything that doesn't match the spec, or that looks broken, blocky, floating, clipped, plain or out of place.
3. Fix what it finds before you stop.
4. Report what it found and what you fixed.
